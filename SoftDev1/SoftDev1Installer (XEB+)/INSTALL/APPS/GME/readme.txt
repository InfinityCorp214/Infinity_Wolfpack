You can paste your icons and configurations in the ART and CFG folders respectively.
The files has the same naming structure and format as the files used in OPL.

Having them in [XEB+'s folder]/GME/ART and [XEB+'s folder]/GME/CFG is not strictly required as they also work from your actual OPL setup from any USB device as mass:/ART and mass:/CFG.

Look at the folders for an example of the files.
They are used to identify the disc-based media.
